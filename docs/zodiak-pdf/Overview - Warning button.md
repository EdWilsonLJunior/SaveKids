> Highlight actions that may have unintended or irreversible consequences.

Warning buttons are for actions that may have **serious consequences** — like deleting data, canceling an account, or making irreversible changes. The warning button comes with a predefined warning icon which **should not be changed or removed**.

> **Warning buttons always require confirmation from the user before completing the action.**

## Anatomy

The button exists with and without an icon.

1. **Container** — houses the content and helps represent the hierarchy and state of the button.
2. **Warning icon**.
3. **Label** — informs what action the button does.

## Variants

Buttons have three variants — **primary, secondary, and tertiary** — to guide users to the main action through a clear button hierarchy.

1. Primary button
2. Secondary button
3. Tertiary button

### Full-width button

**Full-width buttons** span the entire width of their container. They offer high visibility and are easy to interact with, making them ideal for **mobile layouts and fixed-width panels**. The label may change upon interaction, but the button's width should always remain constant.

> *Example: a full-width warning button inside a modal.*
