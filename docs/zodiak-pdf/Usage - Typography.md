# Usage — Typography

> Apply typography tokens consistently to maintain hierarchy and readability across the system.

**Related sections:** [Overview](Overview%20-%20Typography.md) · [Size](Size%20-%20Typography.md) · [Color](Color%20-%20Typography.md)

## Token usage

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Always use text tokens with predefined values for **font size, font weight, and line height** to maintain typographic consistency and design coherence throughout the system. | Don't detach the tokens and modify the font parameters. |

## Width and readability

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Ensure the text content width is **sufficiently wide** to support optimal readability. | Avoid overly narrow layouts that hinder legibility. |

## Alignment

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| **Align text to the left** to promote optimal readability and maintain a consistent visual structure. | Avoid inconsistent or arbitrary text alignments — they can negatively impact readability and disrupt visual flow. |

## Capitalization

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Always use **sentence case**. | Don't use title case. |
