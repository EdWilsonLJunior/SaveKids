# Specs — Show more button

> Reveals more content progressively without navigating away from the page.

**Related sections:** [Overview](Overview%20-%20Show%20more%20button.md) · [Guidelines](Guidelines%20-%20Show%20more%20button.md) · [Button guidelines](Button%20guidelines.md)

## Properties

| Property | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BG Variant** | `onLite` · `onHeavy` · `onPhoto` | `onLite` |
| **Viewport Variant** | Desktop · Desktop small · Tablet large · Tablet · Mobile | Desktop |
| **Hierarchy Variant** | `Secondary` · `Tertiary` | `Tertiary` |

## States

See [Button guidelines](Button%20guidelines.md) for the full five-state specification.

## Size

The show more button adapts to the **viewport**. Icon and typography scale accordingly.

### Icon size

| Variant | Desktop | Desktop small | Tablet large | Tablet | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Secondary** | M | M | M | M | M |
| **Tertiary** | S | S | S | M | S |

### Typography

| Variant | Desktop | Desktop small | Tablet large | Tablet | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Secondary** | `button-text-regular` | `button-text-regular` | `button-text-regular` | `button-text-regular` | `button-text-regular` |
| **Tertiary** | `button-text-small` | `button-text-small` | `button-text-small` | `button-text-regular` | `button-text-small` |

## Placement

The show more button must be placed **centered** below the area where new content will be revealed, in close proximity to that content.
