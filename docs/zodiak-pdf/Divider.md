Dividers help separate content, define boundaries, and improve readability. Use them to organize elements within layouts, such as grouping sections, actions, or items in tables and listings.
Divider lines should be visually subtle and not draw attention. They support structure without overwhelming the interface.

## Variants

Dividers come in two thicknesses: thin and thick.

Each thickness has a primary and secondary variant, distinguished by color. The primary variant is more subtle and preferred for most use cases. The secondary variant uses a slightly darker color and appears more prominent, supporting stronger visual separation when needed.

## Usage

Use dividers to group related content, separate sections and improve visual structure. They are especially helpful in layouts such as tables and listings, where clear separation enhances readability and user understanding.
