## Guidelines

### Usage

When content that has been created by an expert or similar, use the author card to highlight who has written the content. Usually, author information increases the visitors engagement.

If no particular author should be attributed, use the Standard card instead.

### Content

**Author:**

- Use a clear, high-quality image of the author
- Ensure the author's name and role are always present and accurate.
- Use a consistent format for name and title across all cards.

**Button:** Make the label descriptive on what will follow. Don't use "Read more".

### Accessibility

**Semantic structure:**

- Author name as `<h3>` or `<h4>`.
- Role as `<p>` or `<span>` with appropriate ARIA attributes if needed.
- Ensure the full card is clickable.
