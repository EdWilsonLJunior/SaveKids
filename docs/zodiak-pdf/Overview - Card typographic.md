## Variants

### Icon or number

Each card has the option to either show an icon, or a number from 0-9.

- Typographic card with an icon.
- Typographic card with a number.

### Linked cards

All cards can include an optional tertiary button. When present, this button makes the entire card navigable, linking to a relevant section or page.

- A linked Typographic card.
- A Typographic card without a link.
