## Size

Typographic cards are available in two sizes: small and medium. Generally, small cards are used for mobile viewports, while the medium cards are used for all other viewports.

The small card (left) compared to the medium card (right).

The cards automatically expand or contract to fit the available screen width. As the viewport becomes wider, each card expands horizontally; as the viewport narrows, the cards contract.

Example of the responsive behavior of Typographic cards in a composition.

## Used components sizes

These are the sizes of components that are used in within the card.

| Element | Small card | Medium card |
| ------- | ---------- | ----------- |
| Button  | S          | M           |
| Icon    | XL         | XL          |

## Character limit

Refer to the table below for the approximate character limits of each size.

| Element   | Small card | Large card |
| --------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Headline  | ~ 60       | ~ 80       |
| Body text | ~ 120      | ~ 130      |
| Button    | ~ 30       | ~ 30       |

## Placement

Typographic cards can be placed anywhere on a page, except at the very top.

Example placement of the Typographic cards on a page.

## Card groups

Typographic cards can be grouped together to create cohesive sections. Whenever possible, use our premade card compositions.
