Collect multiple selections.

The checkbox component allows users to select multiple options. Unlike radio buttons that limit users to a single choice, checkboxes provide the versatility needed for scenarios where multiple selections are valid and preferred. Checkboxes accommodate a variety of use cases, from settings to complex forms and data filtering.

Anatomy

1. Box: Shows the status of the checkbox.

2. Label: Clearly describes the option associated with the checkbox.

## Variants

Labels may be aligned to the left or right of the checkbox, or hidden entirely in specific use cases.

Checkboxes with hidden labels should only be used in clearly defined, space-constrained contexts where the selection target is immediately understood. This applies when the checkbox is visually tied to a recognizable element, such as selecting an entire card or toggling rows in a data table. Always ensure accessible labeling is provided using aria-label or aria-labelledby to support screen readers.
