## Guidelines

### Usage

The card typographic is used inside the Card grid typographic composition.

Use the card typographic to highlight a small piece of information that does not require imagery.

Ideal for short, focused content.

Linking from cards to more content is possible, but not mandatory

Use the numbered typographic card for a visual list of key steps, or ranked items that should stand out on a page.

### Content

**Icon:** Select icons from the Zodiak icon library that best match your intended purpose.

**Eyebrow:** Eyebrow text should be concise (typically one or two words) and serve as a category or topic indicator. Avoid full sentences or promotional language but focus on clarity and relevance.

**Button:** Make the label descriptive on what will follow. For example, don't use "Read more".
