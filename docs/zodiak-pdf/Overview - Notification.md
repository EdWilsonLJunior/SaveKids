Notifications inform users about important updates, actions, or events within a product. They can:

Provide critical information, such as errors or warnings.

Confirm successful actions.

Alert users to new content or tasks requiring attention.

Notifications are categorized by priority (informational, warning, error) and appear in different formats such as banners or modals, based on urgency and context.

## Variants

Category

Notifications are available in three categories: warning, positive and informative.

Warning notification.

Positive notification.

Information notification.

With or without buttons

Notifications can be displayed with or without buttons. The choice impacts user experience and should align with the importance and urgency of the message.

The variants without a button cannot be dismissed by the user.

No CTA

Use when the message is purely informational and no user action is required. For example for urgent alerts where the only goal is to keep the user informed until resolved.

Single CTA

Encourages interaction without overwhelming the user.
