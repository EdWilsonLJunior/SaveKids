## Guidelines

### Usage

Eyebrows are used exclusively on specific components such as Heros and headline sections. They should never appear as standalone text or outside these contexts.

### Interaction

Eyebrows are static typographic elements and do not support interaction, such as links or hover states.

**Don't:** Don't make the eyebrow a link.

### Content

Eyebrow text should be concise (typically one or two words) and serve as a category or topic indicator. Avoid full sentences or promotional language but focus on clarity and relevance.

#### Do

- Make sure the eyebrow indicates a category or topic.

#### Don't

- Avoid unnecessary or decorative wording in eyebrows.
