# Author

Display the writer(s) of an article.

Figma component

Status

The author component is used to display the author(s) of an article. Including an author to content provides clear attribution, builds trust, and offers context about the source.

Dark mode

1. Avatar: Displays the author’s image. Supports single or multiple avatars when an article has multiple

authors.

2. Name: Always visible; it shows the author’s name(s).

3. Additional information (optional): Depending on the variant, it can include:

Publication date: Indicates when the article was published.

Reading time: Helps readers estimate effort.

Role: Provides context about the author’s expertise.

Company: Adds credibility by showing affiliation.

## Variants

There are three main variants of the Author.

Single author

Used when an article has one author. Displays avatar and name. Can include up to two additional attributes: publication date, reading time, role, or company. It has a maximum of three text rows.

Multi-author

Used when an article has multiple authors. When there are more than three authors, the last avatar indicates the amount of remaining authors.

Brand

If content does not have a single clear author, but is rather general branded content, the brand author can be used. Always display the full name of the brand. Can include the publication date and
reading time, and has a maximum of three text rows.

Multiple authors

On a card

When an article has multiple authors, the multi-author variant is used.

Author card with multiple authors.

On article pages

On the article page itself, when an article has multiple authors, the Type=Brand variant is placed at the top of the article. This variant should always be used in conjunction with the Multi author composition, which displays all the authors in a list. The multi-author composition is always placed at the bottom of the page. This ensures that both at the beginning and the end of the page, the author(s) are accredited.
