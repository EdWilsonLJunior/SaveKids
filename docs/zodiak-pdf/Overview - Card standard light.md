## Variants

### Regular and tall

Typographic cards are available in two main variants: tall and regular sized. Tall cards can be used when more space for images is desired.

The regular sized card (left) compared to the tall card (right).

### Card compositions

The standard cards are used inside the following compositions:

1. **Card grid standard:** Perfect for larger collections, this grid shows up to nine cards at once with an optional "Show more" to reveal additional cards up to a total of eighteen.
2. **Card grid horizontal:** Ideal for avoiding long vertical stacks, this layout displays related cards side-by-side so users can quickly reach content further down the page.
3. **Card grid tall:** Best for showcasing visually strong content, this layout highlights three tall-image cards in a clean horizontal row.
