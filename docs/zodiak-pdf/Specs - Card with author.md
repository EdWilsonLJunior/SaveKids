## Size

The Author card is available in a single responsive size. It automatically adjusts to the viewport and expands or contracts to fit the available layout space.

Example of the responsive behavior of Author cards in a composition.

## Used component sizes

These are the sizes of components that are used in the card.

| Element | Desktop | Desktop small | Tablet | Mobile |
| ------- | ------- | ------------- | ------ | ------ |
| Author  | S       | S             | S      | S      |
| Button  | M       | M             | M      | S      |

## Typography

## Placement

The cards can be placed anywhere on a page, except the very top.

Example placement of the Author cards on a page.
