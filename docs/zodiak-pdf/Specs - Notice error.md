## Placement

Notice can be placed at the top corner of the interface when it is used as a temporary message box that pops up on the screen to give the user feedback.

Desktop and mobile placement examples.

The notice can also be placed within a form, modal or popup when it is used as an error message, callout or information that isn’t temporary (doesn’t disappear and cannot be closed by user) and
requires the user’s action.

Notice within a form.

Character limit

The text in the notice is limited to four lines in total (two lines for the title and two lines for the subtitle).
The exact character limit depends on the component width.
