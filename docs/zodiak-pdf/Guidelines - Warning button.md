# Guidelines — Warning button

> Highlight actions that may have unintended or irreversible consequences.

**Related sections:** [Overview](Overview%20-%20Warning%20button.md) · [Specs (primary)](Specs%20-%20Warning%20button%20primary.md) · [Specs (secondary)](Specs%20-%20Warning%20button%20secundary.md) · [Specs (tertiary)](Specs%20-%20Warning%20button%20tertiary.md)

## Content

### Label

Button labels are crucial for **clearly communicating the action performed**. They should be straightforward, predictable, and descriptive to ensure accessibility for all users, including those with screen readers.

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Use a descriptive button text. | Don't use vague or technical labels. |

### Icons

The warning button comes with a **default icon**. Do not remove or alter the icon.

| ❌ Don't | ❌ Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Don't remove the warning button's icon. | Don't change the warning button's icon. |
